## What are cookies?
Cookies are small files that your browser stores. Similar techniques such as *local storage*, *session storage* and *IndexedDB* also store data in your browser.

## Which cookies do we use?

### Functional (necessary) cookies – no consent required
| Name / technique | Provider | Purpose | Retention |
|---|---|---|---|
| Language preference (`NEXT_LOCALE`) [check] | This website | Remembering your chosen language | [period] |
| Firebase Authentication (IndexedDB/local storage) | Google Firebase | Keeping you signed in | Until you sign out |

| Stripe cookies (including `__stripe_mid`, `__stripe_sid`) | Stripe | Secure payment and fraud prevention at checkout | Up to 1 year / 30 minutes |

### Analytics and marketing cookies
We currently use **no** analytics cookies, tracking cookies or advertising cookies (such as Google Analytics or the Facebook Pixel). If we do so in the future, we will first ask for your consent through a cookie notice and update this policy.

### Embedded content from third parties
If you open an external service (such as a Zoom meeting or an embedded video from a third party [for example YouTube – check]), that party may set its own cookies. That party's cookie policy then applies.

## Managing or deleting cookies
You can always delete or block cookies and stored data through your browser settings. Note: if you block functional cookies, parts such as signing in or paying may no longer work.
